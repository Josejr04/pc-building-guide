Step-by-Step Build Guide

Take your time and work on a clean, flat surface. Touch bare metal on the case first to discharge static electricity.

Build Steps

  1. Install the CPU onto the motherboard (line up the small triangle on the corner).
  2. Install the RAM sticks until they click into place.
  3. Mount the SSD into its slot on the motherboard.
  4. Place the motherboard inside the case.
  5. Install the power supply and plug in its cables.
  6. Install the graphics card in the long slot.
  7. Connect the front panel and fan cables.
  8. Close the case and power on.

After It Turns On

Your first boot goes into a setup screen called the BIOS. Two things to check there:

  • Turn on the memory speed profile by setting XMP Profile = Enabled.
  • Confirm the drive is listed under Boot Priority.
  • Save and exit with the F10 key.

Tip

Take photos as you go. If something does not fit, you can look back at how it was before.
