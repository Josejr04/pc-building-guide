Build Your Own PC

Building a computer sounds hard, but it is really just connecting a handful of parts in the right order. This site walks you through it as a beginner.

Why Build Instead of Buy?

  • Better value: you pay for parts, not for a brand's markup.
  • Full control: you choose every part.
  • Easy upgrades: swap one part later instead of buying a whole new PC.

Where to Start

  1. Read the Parts page to learn what each component does.
  2. Follow the Build Guide in order.
  3. Stuck? Use the advice form.
